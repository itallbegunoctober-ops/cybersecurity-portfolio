# Evidence and Screenshot Guide

Use screenshots and code snippets from your own completed lab sessions. The report text documents the exercises, but original evidence makes the portfolio more convincing.

## Suggested structure

```text
03-wireshark-packet-analysis/
├── README.md
└── evidence/
    ├── 01-pcap-open.png
    ├── 02-ip-filter.png
    └── 03-dns-filter.png
```

## Good evidence to include
- A visible command/filter and relevant output.
- A screenshot of the tool showing the relevant packet, alert or result.
- A short caption explaining what the evidence demonstrates.
- Training completion screens where sharing is permitted.

## Never upload
- Passwords, API keys, access tokens or private keys.
- Personal, customer or employer data.
- Network captures you are not authorized to share.
- Course materials or PCAPs whose terms prohibit redistribution.
- Unredacted private account details.

## Caption examples
- “Used `ls -a` to identify a hidden file in the training environment.”
- “Generated SHA-256 digests and compared saved hash outputs.”
- “Applied a Wireshark display filter to inspect DNS traffic in a sample capture.”
- “Reviewed Suricata alerts generated from a supplied training PCAP.”

Do not claim that a screenshot proves more than it actually shows.
